Messaging lets you exchange private messages with other Nostr users without leaving Porwr. Messages are
end-to-end encrypted and signed by your active key inside the extension.

## What Messaging is for

Use Messaging to:

- send and receive private messages with your contacts
- find a contact by name, NIP-05 identifier, or npub and start a conversation
- see which conversations have unread messages
- review message requests from people outside your contacts
- message the Diogel support team.

## How private messages work

Porwr uses NIP-17 private direct messages.

- The message content is encrypted with NIP-44 so only you and the recipient can read it.
- Each message is sealed and gift-wrapped, so relays cannot see who sent it, who it is for, or what it says.
- Messages are delivered to the recipient's **Direct Message Relays** (a kind `10050` relay list), and replies come back to yours.

Relays can still see that an encrypted event arrived for a public key. They cannot read it.

## Before you start: Direct Message Relays

Nobody can send you a private message until you have published Direct Message Relays.

If you have none, Messaging shows a notice with a **Set up relays** button that opens **Relay Management**. See
[Direct Message Relays](/docs/porwr/relay#direct-message-relays) for how to choose them.

Other people need Direct Message Relays too. If a contact has not published any, Porwr tells you they cannot
receive private messages yet.

## Conversations

![Messaging, with a conversation open and an unread badge on another chat](/img/screenshots/porwr/messaging.png)

The **Conversations** list has two tabs.

### Chats

Chats lists the people you are talking to. Each entry shows the latest message, and a badge shows how many
unread messages are waiting.

Choose a conversation to open it. To take someone off the list, use the remove button beside their name. This
only removes the conversation from Chats; it does not delete messages from relays.

### Requests

Requests lists people outside your contacts who have written to you. Reply to someone to move them to Chats.

![The Requests tab, with a message from someone outside your contacts](/img/screenshots/porwr/messaging-requests.png)

## Starting a conversation

1. Open **Messaging**.
2. In **Search contacts**, type a name, NIP-05 identifier, or npub.
3. Select the contact and choose **Message**.
4. Write your message and press **Enter** to send. **Shift+Enter** adds a new line.

If a message cannot be sent, it is marked **Not sent.** and you can **Retry** it.

## Contacts

Messaging uses your contacts, the people you follow in your NIP-02 follow list.

Porwr loads your contacts and their profiles in the background and keeps them in a local cache. This happens
when the extension starts and when you switch account, so Messaging and Contact Management open quickly
instead of waiting for every profile to load from relays.

### Contact Management

Use **Contact Management** to browse and edit the active account's follow list.

![Contact Management, with three contacts](/img/screenshots/porwr/contacts.png)

- **Add Contact** searches by profile name, npub, hex public key, or NIP-05 identifier.
- **Edit contact** changes a contact's petname or relay URL.
- **Remove contact** takes a contact off the list.
- **Publish Changes** signs and publishes the updated follow list.

Publishing replaces the active account's whole NIP-02 follow list with the contacts shown, so check the list
before you publish.

Profile searches use your **Profile Search Relays**, which you can change in
[Relay Management](/docs/porwr/relay#profile-search-relays).

## Support

The **Support** menu opens a private chat with the Diogel support team inside Porwr. It used to open the
GitHub issues page.

![The Support page, with the relay reminder and an empty chat with Diogel Support](/img/screenshots/porwr/support-chat.png)

Support messages are NIP-17 private messages, like any other conversation. Your sent messages and the team's
replies stay in the Support chat.

Diogel Support replies to your Direct Message Relays. Choose relays that keep private messages, such as
`wss://nos.lol` or `wss://relay.primal.net`. Some relays accept private messages but do not keep them, so
replies sent there never arrive.

Never send your private key, vault password, or NWC connection string to support, or to anyone else.

## Privacy notes

- Message content is encrypted end to end. Porwr never sends your messages to a Diogel server.
- Gift wrapping hides the sender, recipient, and timing of each message from relays.
- Your contact list is public Nostr data. Your conversations are not.
- The contact and profile cache is stored locally in the extension.

## Common issues

### Nobody can message me

You have no Direct Message Relays, or the relays you chose do not keep private messages. Open
**Relay Management** and check **Direct Message Relays**.

### A contact cannot receive messages

The contact has not published Direct Message Relays. They need to set them up in their own Nostr client.

### Could not check for new messages

Your relays did not respond. Porwr tries again shortly.

### Contacts take a while to appear

The first load fetches your follow list and profiles from relays. Later visits use the cache.

## Best practices

- Publish 1 to 3 Direct Message Relays that keep private messages.
- Check the active account before sending. Messages are signed by the active key.
- Reply to a request only if you want that person in your Chats.
- Never share secrets in a message, even an encrypted one.
