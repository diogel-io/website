This feature is based on NIP-47. It lets Porwr talk to an external Lightning wallet service through Nostr relays.

## Important safety summary

- Porwr does not become a full Lightning wallet.
- Porwr does not custody your funds.
- Porwr stores NWC connection secrets inside the encrypted vault.
- Websites can use your wallet only through WebLN, after you enable it for that site.
- Every payment, from the extension or from a website, requires explicit approval.

## What Wallet Management is for

Use Wallet Management to:

- connect a wallet, either with a few clicks or by pasting an NWC URI
- label wallet connections and choose the active one
- store connection secrets in the encrypted vault
- fetch wallet capabilities
- check wallet balance
- pay BOLT11 invoices after explicit approval
- review payment history

## What Nostr Wallet Connect is

Nostr Wallet Connect lets an app send wallet commands over Nostr events.

Porwr holds a connection secret for your wallet service and uses it to send encrypted wallet requests over the
wallet's relays.

The wallet service decides which commands are allowed.

## Connecting a wallet

Open **Wallet Management** and choose **Connect wallet**.

### Connect with Alby Hub or Coinos

For Alby Hub and Coinos you don't need to copy anything.

1. Choose **Connect Alby Hub** or **Connect Coinos**.
2. Approve Porwr in the wallet window that opens.
3. Porwr stores the connection in the encrypted vault.

The connection key is created inside Porwr and is never shown to websites.

If you run your own Alby Hub, turn on **Use my own Alby Hub** and enter its `https://` address first.

If the wallet window doesn't open, allow pop-ups for Porwr and try again.

### Paste a connection string

For any other Nostr Wallet Connect wallet, such as LNbits with NWC support, use
**Paste a connection string instead**.

1. In your wallet service, find **Apps**, **Connections**, **Developer**, or **Nostr Wallet Connect**.
2. Create a new app connection and copy the full `nostr+walletconnect://...` URI.
3. In Porwr, enter an optional **Connection label**, such as `Alby`, `LNbits`, or `Home node`.
4. Paste the URI into **NWC URI**.
5. Choose **Import connection**.

Porwr validates the URI and stores the secret in the encrypted vault.

An NWC URI contains the wallet service public key, relay URLs, a client secret and, optionally, a Lightning
address (`lud16`). Treat the full URI as sensitive. Do not share it publicly.

## Active wallet

If you have more than one connection, one is the active wallet. Porwr uses it for payments, including WebLN
payments requested by websites.

Other connections are listed under **Other connections**. Use **Make active** to switch.

## Configured connections

After connecting, Porwr shows each connection with:

- local label
- wallet service public key preview
- client public key preview
- configured relays
- advertised capabilities after fetching info
- last balance check when available

Connection summaries intentionally do not show the client secret.

## Wallet info

Click **Info** to ask the wallet service for its metadata and capabilities.

Capabilities may include:

- `get_info`
- `get_balance`
- `pay_invoice`
- `make_invoice`
- `lookup_invoice`
- `list_transactions`

The exact list depends on the wallet service and the permissions granted to the NWC connection.

## Balance checks

Click **Balance** to send a NIP-47 `get_balance` request.

If successful, Porwr shows the balance in sats.

If it fails, check:

- wallet service is online
- relays are reachable
- NWC connection is still valid
- wallet permits `get_balance`
- browser network access is working

## Paying invoices

Porwr supports explicit user-approved `pay_invoice` requests.

Workflow:

1. Click **Info** for the wallet connection.
2. Confirm `pay_invoice` appears in capabilities.
3. Click **Pay invoice**.
4. Paste a BOLT11 invoice.
5. Review parsed amount and invoice preview.
6. Tick the acknowledgement checkbox.
7. Click **Approve and pay**.

Start with a tiny test invoice, such as 1-10 sats.

## Why Pay invoice may be disabled

The Pay invoice button is disabled unless the wallet advertises `pay_invoice`.

If it is disabled:

1. click **Info**
2. check whether `pay_invoice` appears
3. verify the wallet connection grants payment permission

## Payment approval dialog

The approval dialog is intentionally direct because Lightning payments are real money.

It shows:

- selected wallet connection
- BOLT11 invoice field
- parsed amount where possible
- shortened invoice preview
- explicit acknowledgement checkbox
- final **Approve and pay** action.

Porwr only sends the wallet payment command after final approval.

## Payment history

Payment history is stored inside the encrypted vault.

It records the latest 100 payment attempts.

Entries include:

- timestamp
- wallet connection label
- status: succeeded or failed
- parsed amount when available
- fees when returned
- short payment hash when returned
- invoice preview
- error message for failed attempts

Porwr does not show the payment preimage in the UI.

## Zaps from Nostr clients

Porwr 0.3.0 lets you zap from Nostr web clients such as Snort, Coracle, and nostrudel. The client builds the
zap. Porwr signs it and pays it, and asks for your approval at each step.

A zap from a website goes like this:

1. **The client reads your relays.** It calls `window.nostr.getRelays()` to fill in the zap request.
2. **You approve the zap request.** The client asks Porwr to sign a NIP-57 zap request (kind `9734`). The
   approval shows the amount, the recipient's npub, the note or event being zapped, and any comment. Signing
   attaches your identity to the zap. It does not pay anything. You can approve it **Just this once** or
   **For the next 8 hours**, but not always.
3. **You enable WebLN for the site.** The first time, the site calls `window.webln.enable()` and Porwr asks
   whether to let it request payments from your active wallet. Enabling pays nothing by itself.
4. **You approve the payment.** The site calls `window.webln.sendPayment(invoice)`. Porwr shows the invoice
   amount and asks you to approve it. A payment approval is never remembered, so every zap asks again.

![A zap request approval, showing the amount, recipient, zapped event and comment](/img/screenshots/porwr/zap-approval.png){width="360"}

Porwr then pays the invoice through your active wallet connection, and the recipient's wallet publishes the zap
receipt.

### What Porwr does not do

- Porwr does not start zaps itself. There is no "send zap" button in the extension.
- It does not look up Lightning addresses, fetch invoices, or keep a zap history. Those are the client's job.
- It supports the WebLN methods `enable`, `getInfo`, and `sendPayment`. Other methods, such as `keysend` and
  `makeInvoice`, are rejected with an `UnsupportedMethodError`.

### Another WebLN extension

If another extension, such as Alby, already provides `window.webln`, Porwr leaves it in place. Porwr's own
WebLN is then available as `window.diogelWebln`.

### Breaking change for developers

Porwr 0.3.0 removes `window.nostr.diogel.getZapCapabilities` and `window.nostr.diogel.sendZap`. Sites that
used them should build the zap request themselves, sign it with `window.nostr.signEvent`, and pay the invoice
with `window.webln.sendPayment`.

Zap history saved by an earlier version is dropped the next time the vault is saved.

## Removing a connection

Use **Remove** to delete a wallet connection from the vault.

You should also revoke unused or exposed NWC connections from the wallet service itself. Removing the local copy from Porwr does not necessarily revoke the credential on the wallet provider side.

## Security guidance

- Treat NWC URIs like API keys.
- Do not post NWC URIs in support tickets, screenshots, chats, or logs.
- Revoke exposed wallet connections immediately.
- Use low limits where your wallet service supports them.
- Start with small test payments.
- Do not approve invoices you do not understand.
- Enable WebLN only for sites you trust, and check the amount on every payment approval.
- Remove old wallet connections you no longer use.

## Troubleshooting

### Import fails

Check that the URI is complete and starts with `nostr+walletconnect://`.

It must include at least one relay and a valid secret.

### Info times out

Wallet service or relays may be offline. Try again later or check the wallet service.

### Balance times out

Check relay connectivity, wallet service status, and whether the NWC connection is still valid.

### Payment fails

Possible causes:

- invoice expired
- wallet has insufficient balance
- wallet refuses payment
- NWC connection lacks `pay_invoice` permission
- relays are unreachable
- wallet service is offline.

Failed attempts should appear in payment history with an error where available.
