This page describes Symudol 1.0.0. Every feature on it, including biometric unlock, backup and wipe, is in that release.

## What the vault is

The vault is where Symudol keeps your Nostr identities on the phone. It is protected by a 6-digit PIN.

Your PIN is local to this phone. It is not an online account, and nobody can reset it for you. If you forget it, only a [backup](#back-up-your-vault), or your own copy of each private key, brings your identities back.

## Create your PIN

The first time you open Symudol, you see the **Local Access PIN** card: "Create a local access PIN for Symudol."

1. Under **Create a security PIN**, enter 6 digits on the keypad.
2. Under **Confirm your PIN**, enter the same 6 digits again.
3. Symudol creates the vault and opens the **Accounts** screen.

If the two PINs differ, you see "PINs do not match. Try again." and start over.

The PIN must be exactly 6 digits. Use the backspace key to correct a digit.

Next, add an identity, or restore one from a backup. See [Accounts and profile](/docs/symudol/accounts) and [Restore from a backup](#restore-from-a-backup).

## Unlock the vault

When the vault is locked, Symudol shows **Unlock Vault** and "Enter security PIN to continue".

![The Unlock Vault screen](/img/screenshots/symudol/unlock.png)

1. Enter your 6-digit PIN.
2. Symudol unlocks as soon as the sixth digit is entered. You can also tap **Unlock Vault**.

If the PIN is wrong, you see "Invalid PIN. Please try again."

If [biometric unlock](#biometric-unlock) is on, you can use your fingerprint or face instead.

### Too many wrong PINs

Symudol makes you wait longer after each run of wrong PINs:

| Wrong PINs in a row | Wait |
| --- | --- |
| 1 to 4 | None |
| 5 | 30 seconds |
| 6 | 1 minute |
| 7 | 5 minutes |
| 8 | 15 minutes |
| 9 | 1 hour |
| 10 | 2 hours |
| 11 | 4 hours |
| 12 | 8 hours |
| 13 | 16 hours |
| 14 or more | 24 hours |

While you wait, you see "Too many failed attempts. Try again in" followed by the time left, with a countdown. The keypad does not work until the countdown ends.

- Changing the phone's date or time does not shorten the wait.
- If you restart the phone during a wait, the wait starts again from the beginning.
- A correct PIN, or a biometric unlock, resets the count.
- Wrong PINs entered in Change PIN, Backup Vault or when turning on biometric unlock count too.

By default, wrong PINs never erase the vault. You can change that with [Erase after failed PIN attempts](#erase-after-failed-pin-attempts).

### Session expired

If the vault locked itself because you were inactive, the screen title is **Session Expired** with "Your session has timed out due to inactivity". Enter your PIN to continue.

### A request is waiting

If another app sends a request while the vault is locked, the unlock screen shows:

> An external Android app is waiting for a NIP-55 signing decision. Unlock to review it, or cancel the request.

- Unlock to see the request on the **Requests** tab.
- Tap **Cancel external request** to refuse it.

If you do neither, the request is cancelled after 5 minutes.

## Forgot your PIN

Symudol cannot recover or reset your PIN. Your keys are encrypted with it, and Symudol keeps no copy.

Tap **Forgot PIN?** on the unlock screen. The **Forgot your PIN?** dialog offers:

- **Restore from a backup**. Symudol erases this vault, you create a new PIN, and Symudol then asks for your backup file. See [Restore from a backup](#restore-from-a-backup).
- **Erase and start again**. Symudol erases this vault and you set it up from scratch.

Both choices erase the vault first, and you confirm by typing WIPE, as in [Wipe the vault](#wipe-the-vault). Any key you have not backed up elsewhere is lost.

## Biometric unlock

Biometric unlock lets you open the vault with your fingerprint or face instead of the PIN. It is off until you turn it on.

### Turn it on

1. Open [Settings](/docs/symudol/settings) and turn on **Biometric Unlock**.
2. In **Turn on biometric unlock**, enter your PIN and tap **Continue**.
3. Confirm with your fingerprint or face when Android asks.

You see "Biometric unlock is on". Turn the switch off to stop using it.

Symudol needs a strong fingerprint or face sensor. If it cannot be turned on, the setting says why:

- "Add a fingerprint or face in Android settings first"
- "This phone has no strong biometric sensor"
- "Not available on this phone"

### Unlock with your fingerprint or face

When the unlock screen opens, Symudol asks for your fingerprint or face straight away. You can also tap the fingerprint key on the keypad. To type your PIN instead, tap **Use PIN**.

Biometric unlock works even while you are waiting after too many wrong PINs.

### When the PIN is still needed

- After the phone restarts.
- 72 hours after you last entered your PIN.

Symudol then says "Enter your PIN: biometric unlock needs it after a restart or every 72 hours."

If you add or change a fingerprint or face in Android settings, Symudol turns biometric unlock off and shows "Biometric unlock was turned off because this phone's fingerprints or face changed. Unlock with your PIN." Turn it on again in Settings if you want it.

Changing your PIN keeps biometric unlock on. Wiping the vault turns it off.

## Change your PIN

1. In [Settings](/docs/symudol/settings), tap **Change PIN**.
2. **Enter your current PIN**.
3. **Enter a new PIN**.
4. **Confirm the new PIN**.

You see "PIN changed". Your identities and Trusted app permissions are kept.

- If the new PIN is the same as the old one, you see "Choose a different PIN."
- If the current PIN is wrong, you see "That is not your current PIN." and start again.
- If the two new PINs differ, you see "The PINs do not match. Enter the new PIN again."

If the change is interrupted, your old PIN still works.

## Back up your vault

A backup is a file that holds every identity in the vault, encrypted with a passphrase you choose. If you lose your phone or forget your PIN, it is the only way to get your identities back, unless you kept your own copy of each private key to [import](/docs/symudol/accounts) again.

::warning
Anyone with the backup file and its passphrase controls your identities. Symudol can't recover a forgotten passphrase. Keep the file and the passphrase somewhere safe, and apart.
::

1. In [Settings](/docs/symudol/settings), tap **Backup Vault**.
2. Under **Make a backup**, enter a **Passphrase** of at least 12 characters. Four or more random words are strong and easy to type. Use a different passphrase from your PIN.
3. Enter it again in **Confirm passphrase**.
4. Tap **Back up and share**, then enter your PIN.
5. Choose where to save the file from the Android share sheet, for example a cloud drive, a USB drive or a message to yourself.

You see "Backup made. Keep the file and its passphrase somewhere safe, and apart." If you close the share sheet without saving, the backup is not kept, and you need to back up again.

The file is named `symudol-backup-YYYY-MM-DD.json`. It holds each identity's name and its private key, encrypted with your passphrase in the NIP-49 (`ncryptsec`) format.

The backup does not hold your Trusted app permissions or your settings. Set these up again after a restore.

Back up again after you add an identity.

## Restore from a backup

Restoring adds the identities from a backup file to this vault. You can restore:

- From **Restore from a backup** on the **Backup Vault** screen in Settings.
- From the **Accounts** screen when the vault has no identities.
- After **Forgot PIN?**, once you have set a new PIN.

To restore:

1. Tap **Restore from a backup**.
2. Pick the backup file.
3. Enter the passphrase the backup was made with, then tap **Restore**.

You see **Backup restored** and how many identities were added. Identities that are already in the vault are kept and skipped.

If the restore fails, you see **Couldn't restore** with the reason. The most common are:

- "That passphrase doesn't open this backup."
- "This file is not a Symudol backup."
- "This backup is damaged."
- "This backup was made by a newer Symudol. Update Symudol to restore it."

## Erase after failed PIN attempts

You can have Symudol erase the vault after too many wrong PINs in a row. This protects your keys if someone keeps guessing your PIN, but a forgotten PIN then erases them too.

::warning
Make a [backup](#back-up-your-vault) before you turn this on. An erase cannot be undone.
::

1. In [Settings](/docs/symudol/settings), under SECURITY, tap **Erase after failed PIN attempts**.
2. Choose **10 failed attempts**, **15 failed attempts** or **20 failed attempts**.
3. Read the warning and tap **Turn on**.

Symudol does not ask for your PIN, because the vault is already unlocked. It is **Off** by default. Choose **Off** to turn it off again.

When you are 3 or fewer wrong PINs from the limit, the unlock and Change PIN screens warn you, for example "3 attempts left before Symudol erases all keys."

At the limit, Symudol [wipes the vault](#wipe-the-vault) and shows "Too many failed PIN attempts. Symudol erased all keys."

The waits in [Too many wrong PINs](#too-many-wrong-pins) still apply.

## Wipe the vault

Wiping erases everything Symudol keeps on this phone. Use it before you give the phone away, or to start again.

1. In [Settings](/docs/symudol/settings), tap **Wipe Vault**.
2. Read the list of what will be deleted.
3. Type WIPE in **Type WIPE to confirm**.
4. Tap **Wipe vault**.

A wipe permanently deletes:

- Every identity and its private key.
- Your PIN.
- Every Trusted app permission.
- Biometric unlock.
- Your vault settings, such as the lock times.

It cannot be undone. Symudol keeps no copy, so any key you have not backed up elsewhere is gone for good.

After the wipe, Symudol shows the PIN setup screen. If the phone stops during a wipe, Symudol finishes it the next time it opens.

## How your keys are protected

- Your PIN is never stored. Symudol turns it into an encryption key with Argon2id, a deliberately slow function that makes guessing PINs expensive.
- That key unlocks a random vault key.
- Each private key is encrypted with the vault key using AES-256-GCM.
- The encrypted data is kept in Android's secure storage on the phone.
- If biometric unlock is on, a copy of the vault key is protected by a key in the Android Keystore that only works after a fingerprint or face check.
- Symudol never uploads or syncs your keys. Android's own app backup and device transfer do not copy the vault.
- Android blocks screenshots and screen recording of Symudol.

When the vault locks, Symudol clears the vault key from memory. Nothing can be signed, encrypted or decrypted until you unlock again.

## When the vault locks

The vault locks:

- When you tap **Lock Vault** in [Settings](/docs/symudol/settings).
- After a period without use while the app is open. The default is 5 minutes.
- After the app has been in the background for a while. The default is 5 minutes.

You can change both times in [Settings](/docs/symudol/settings).

Symudol does not lock for being in the background while it is in the middle of a request from another app. It also waits while you pick a file or an image, or share a backup. This stops the vault locking halfway through.
