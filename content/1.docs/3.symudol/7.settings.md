This page describes Symudol 1.0.0.

## Settings

Open the **Settings** tab at the bottom of the screen. Settings are grouped under **SECURITY**, **NETWORK** and **VAULT**.

## Security

### Change PIN

"Update your security access code". Enter your current PIN, then the new one twice. See [Change your PIN](/docs/symudol/vault#change-your-pin).

### Biometric Unlock

A switch that lets you unlock with your fingerprint or face. Its description shows whether it can be used, for example "Add a fingerprint or face in Android settings first" or "This phone has no strong biometric sensor". See [Biometric unlock](/docs/symudol/vault#biometric-unlock).

### Erase after failed PIN attempts

Erases the vault after too many wrong PINs in a row. Choose **Off**, **10 failed attempts**, **15 failed attempts** or **20 failed attempts**. The default is **Off**. See [Erase after failed PIN attempts](/docs/symudol/vault#erase-after-failed-pin-attempts).

### In-app inactivity timeout

Locks the vault when you stop using Symudol while it is open. The description shows your choice, for example "Locks when you stop using Symudol while it is open: 5 minutes".

Choose from **Never while app is open**, **1 minute**, **5 minutes**, **15 minutes**, **30 minutes** or **1 hour**. The default is 5 minutes.

Touching the screen resets the timer. When it runs out, you see the **Session Expired** screen and enter your PIN again.

### Background lock delay

Locks the vault after Symudol has been sent to the background.

Choose from **Immediately**, **1 minute**, **5 minutes**, **15 minutes**, **30 minutes**, **1 hour** or **Never while app is running**. The default is 5 minutes.

Symudol does not lock this way while it is handling a request from another app, or while you pick a file or an image or share a backup. This stops the vault locking halfway through.

### Trusted Nostr apps

"Review remembered NIP-55 app permissions. They apply whenever the vault is unlocked." A remembered decision to share your public key, or a remembered rejection, is also answered while the vault is locked.

Opens the list of remembered decisions for apps on your phone, where you can revoke them. See [Trusted Nostr apps](/docs/symudol/approving-requests#trusted-nostr-apps).

### Session security

An information note: "Shorter delays are safer; longer delays are more convenient. Never only lasts while the app process remains running."

If Android closes Symudol, the vault is locked the next time you open it, whatever you chose.

### Open-source licences

"Licences for Symudol and the packages it uses". Opens the licences of Symudol and every package it is built with. Symudol itself is released under the MIT licence.

### Privacy policy

"What Symudol stores and sends; opens in your browser". Opens the [Symudol privacy policy](/privacy/symudol) on diogel.io. If no browser opens, Symudol shows the address instead.

### Lock Vault

"Secure your vault immediately". Locks the vault at once. You need your PIN, or biometric unlock if it is on, to unlock it again.

## Network

### Profile relays and media server

"Where profiles are read from and published to, and the media server for image uploads". Opens **Relays and media server**, which has three parts:

- **PROFILE RELAYS**: profiles are read from and published to these. At least one is needed.
- **INDEXER RELAYS**: these collect profiles and relay lists from across the network. Symudol sends them your relay list when you save it, but never your profile.
- **MEDIA SERVER**: the **Blossom server** that Edit profile uploads pictures and banners to, when an identity lists no Blossom server of its own.

The defaults are:

| Setting | Default |
|---|---|
| Profile relays | `wss://relay.damus.io`, `wss://nos.lol`, `wss://relay.primal.net` |
| Indexer relays | `wss://purplepag.es`, `wss://profiles.nostr1.com`, `wss://relay.nos.social` |
| Blossom server | `https://blossom.primal.net` |

Each list holds at most 8 relays. Tap **Save** to keep your changes, and you see "Relay settings saved". Tap **Reset to defaults** to go back to the list above.

If an address is not accepted, Symudol says why:

- "Enter a relay address starting with ws:// or wss://"
- "That relay is already in the list"
- "A list holds at most 8 relays"
- "Enter a server address starting with https://"

Every relay Symudol asks sees the identity's public key and your IP address. Symudol asks them only when you load or edit a profile or relay list. See [Which relays Symudol asks](/docs/symudol/accounts#which-relays-symudol-asks).

## Vault

### Backup Vault

"Export your recovery data". Makes an encrypted backup file of every identity, and restores identities from one. See [Back up your vault](/docs/symudol/vault#back-up-your-vault) and [Restore from a backup](/docs/symudol/vault#restore-from-a-backup).

### Wipe Vault

"Permanently delete all keys". Erases every identity, your PIN and your settings from this phone, after you type WIPE to confirm. See [Wipe the vault](/docs/symudol/vault#wipe-the-vault).
