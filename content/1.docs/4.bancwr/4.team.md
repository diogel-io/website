## The Team page

The **Team** page lists everyone registered with the bunker. Only administrators can open it.

![The Team Management page, with the member list and the Add Team Member form](/img/screenshots/bancwr/team.png)

Each row shows the member's name, their public key as an `npub`, their role, and a button to remove them.

## Roles

| Role | What they can do |
|------|------------------|
| **Administrator** | Everything: the team, connection tokens, all connected apps, the signing log and the bunker's configuration. |
| **User** | See the bunker's health. Edit their own profile, follows and relays. See and revoke the apps connected for them. |
| **Signer** | See the bunker's health. Edit their own profile. |

See [What is Bancwr](/docs/bancwr) for the full list of pages each role can open.

## Adding a member

1. Open **Team**.
2. Under **Add Team Member**, enter a **Name** to recognise them by.
3. Enter their **Pubkey**, as an `npub` or a hex public key.
4. Choose a **Role**: Administrator, User or Signer. The default is Signer.
5. Click **Add Member**.

The member can sign in straight away with that key.

Ask people for their public key (`npub`). Never ask for a private key (`nsec`).

### Keys that are refused

The bunker refuses to add:

- A key that is already registered, whether it was entered as an `npub` or as hex.
- The bunker's own key. It can never be registered, with any role.
- A value that is not a valid public key.

When a key is refused, Bancwr shows **Failed to add member**.

## Removing a member

1. Open **Team**.
2. Click the remove button on the member's row.
3. Confirm.

Removing a member takes effect at once. They lose access on their next page load.

Removing a member also ends their unused connection tokens and the app connections made for them.

### The last administrator

You cannot remove the only administrator. The bunker would be left with nobody able to manage it. Add another administrator first.

When a removal is refused, Bancwr shows **Failed to remove member**.

## Changing a role

There is no option to change a member's role. Remove the member and add them again with the new role.

Removing a member ends the app connections made for them, so those apps need new connection tokens afterwards.
