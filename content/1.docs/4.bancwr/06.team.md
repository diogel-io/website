## The Team page

The **Team** page lists everyone registered with the bunker.

- Administrators see it as **Team Management**, and can add members, change their roles and remove them.
- Viewers see the list read-only.
- Signers do not have this page.

![The Team Management page, with the member list and the Add Team Member form](/img/screenshots/bancwr/team.png)

Each row shows the member's name, their public key as an `npub`, and their role. For administrators, the role is a select, and each row has a remove button. Click a member's name to open their profile, read-only, from their own relays.

## Roles

| Role | What they can do |
|------|------------------|
| **Admin** | Everything: the team, identities, connection tokens, approvals, every connected app, the signing log and its export, and the bunker's configuration. Also everything a signer and a viewer can do. |
| **Signer** | Have apps connected for them. See their own connected apps and their own recent signatures. See their own requests waiting for approval. Edit their own profile, follows and relays. |
| **Viewer** | See the bunker's health, and the team and each member's profile, read-only. A viewer never holds an app connection. |

### Which pages each role sees

| Page | Admin | Signer | Viewer |
|------|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| Dashboard | Metrics, health and everyone's activity | Health, their connected apps and their own signatures | Health, and a link to the team |
| Team | Yes | | Read-only |
| A member's profile | Yes | | Yes |
| Config | Yes | | |
| Logs | Yes | | |
| Connections | All apps | Their own apps | |
| Approvals | All requests, and deciding them | Their own requests, to see | |
| Profile, Follows, Relays | Yes | Yes | |

The sidebar shows only the pages a role can open. The bunker checks the same rules on every request.

## Adding a member

1. Open **Team**.
2. Under **Add Team Member**, enter a **Name** to recognise them by.
3. Enter their **Pubkey**, as an `npub` or a hex public key.
4. Choose a **Role**: Admin, Signer or Viewer. The default is Signer.
5. If the bunker holds more than one identity, tick under **May sign as** the identities they may sign as. The primary is ticked to start with. Viewers sign as none, so this is not shown for them.
6. Click **Add Member**.

The member can sign in straight away with that key.

Ask people for their public key (`npub`). Never ask for a private key (`nsec`).

### Keys that are refused

The bunker refuses to add:

- A key that is already registered, whether it was entered as an `npub` or as hex.
- The bunker's own key, or the key of any identity it holds. These can never be registered, with any role.
- A value that is not a valid public key.

When a key is refused, Bancwr shows **Failed to add member**.

## Changing a role

An administrator changes a member's role in one step, without removing them.

1. Open **Team**.
2. Choose the new role in the member's **Role** select.

Bancwr shows that the member is now in the new role, such as **Alice is now Signer**. The change takes effect on the member's next request.

- Making a member a viewer disconnects the apps connected for them, and revokes their unused connection tokens. Bancwr asks you to confirm first. If they sign again later, issue them new tokens.
- The last administrator cannot be demoted. Bancwr shows **The last administrator cannot be demoted**. Make someone else an admin first.

Every role change is recorded in the audit log, with who made it.

## Choosing which identities a member may sign as

When the bunker holds more than one identity, each signer needs a grant for every identity they may sign as, the primary included. See [Signing identities](/docs/bancwr/signing-identities).

1. Open **Team** and click the member's name.
2. Under **May sign as**, tick the identities they may sign as, and untick the ones they may not.

- Administrators may sign as every identity, with no grant needed.
- Removing a grant disconnects their apps for that identity, and revokes their unused tokens for it. Their other identities are untouched.
- With only one identity, **May sign as** does not appear, and everyone who can sign may sign as it.

## Removing a member

1. Open **Team**.
2. Click the remove button on the member's row.
3. Confirm.

Removing a member takes effect at once. They lose access on their next request.

Removing a member also ends their unused connection tokens and the app connections made for them.

### The last administrator

You cannot remove the only administrator. The bunker would be left with nobody able to manage it. Add another administrator first.

When a removal is refused, Bancwr shows **Failed to remove member**.

## Upgrading from an early build

Early builds had the roles Administrator, User and Signer. The first time a bunker with the new roles starts, such as 0.1.0, it moves members to them once, least privilege first:

- User becomes Signer, and keeps their pages and connected apps.
- The old Signer becomes Viewer, and loses their app connections and unused tokens.
- Administrator stays Admin.

The bunker's log names every member whose role changed, and every connection it revoked.
