## The first administrator

Only keys registered with the bunker can use the dashboard. Only an administrator can register keys. So the first administrator comes from the environment, through `BANCWR_ADMIN_PUBKEY`.

Set `BANCWR_ADMIN_PUBKEY` in `.env` to your own `npub` (or hex public key), then start or restart the bunker.

At startup:

- If no administrator is registered, the bunker registers that key as an administrator. If the key is already a member, it promotes it to administrator. The bunker log says which.
- Once any administrator exists, the setting does nothing. It never re-adds a key you removed, and it never demotes anyone.

You can leave `BANCWR_ADMIN_PUBKEY` set, but it is tidier to remove it once you have signed in.

The bunker refuses to start if `BANCWR_ADMIN_PUBKEY` is not a valid key, or if it is the bunker's own key.

### Why the bunker's key can never sign in

The bunker's own key (`BUNKER_NSEC`) can never sign in to the dashboard. It can never be registered as a member either, with any role.

The bunker signs for other people over NIP-46. If its key could sign in, anyone the bunker signs for could get it to sign a Bancwr sign-in and act as that key. Signing in with it would also mean putting the server's signing key into a browser extension.

If you try to sign in with the bunker's key, Bancwr refuses and tells you to sign in with your own key.

### If no administrator is set

If you start Bancwr without `BANCWR_ADMIN_PUBKEY`, and no administrator is registered:

- The bunker logs a warning at every start.
- The health indicator shows the **Administrator** check as **Needs attention**.
- Anyone who signs in sees a page saying the bunker has no administrator yet, with their `npub`.

To make that key the first administrator:

1. Copy the `npub` shown on the page.
2. Set `BANCWR_ADMIN_PUBKEY` to it in `.env`.
3. Restart the bunker.
4. Sign in again.

## Signing in

Open Bancwr at the address set in `NUXT_SITE_ORIGIN`. The **Sign in** page offers two ways in.

### With a browser extension (NIP-07)

1. Click **Sign in with extension**.
2. Your extension tells Bancwr which key it holds.
3. Confirm the key (see below).
4. Approve the sign-in request in your extension.

If no extension is found, the page says so and suggests a remote signer instead.

### With a remote signer (NIP-46)

1. Paste your signer's `bunker://` string into **Connection string**.
2. Click **Connect and sign in**.
3. Approve the connection in your signer. If your signer asks, use the **Approve in your signer** link.
4. Confirm the key (see below).

Click **Cancel** to stop waiting for the signer.

### Confirming the key

Before anything is signed, Bancwr shows the key your signer chose, under **Sign in as**. It shows the key's name and picture from its public profile when it can find them, and the `npub`.

- Click **Continue as …** to sign in with that key.
- Click **Use another key** if it is not the key you meant. Nothing has been signed.

If the signer then signs with a different key than the one you confirmed, the sign-in is refused.

### Switching keys in your extension

Your extension decides which key signs in. Some extensions, such as Porwr, keep using the key a site first connected with, whichever key is selected.

To sign in with another key:

1. Open your extension's connected sites.
2. Disconnect Bancwr's address.
3. Choose the key you want.
4. Sign in again.

With a remote signer, paste the connection string of the signer that holds the key you want.

## Keys that are not registered

If you sign in with a key that is not registered, you see **This key is not registered with this bunker**.

The page shows your `npub`, with a button to copy it. Send it to an administrator and ask them to add you on the **Team** page.

Click **Sign out and try another key** to go back to the sign-in page.

## Signing out

Use the sign-out button in the sidebar footer, next to your name and role.

Signing out ends your session everywhere it was used. Restarting the dashboard container signs everyone out.

## Common issues

### The address does not match

Sign-in is refused if the page's address does not match `NUXT_SITE_ORIGIN`. Open Bancwr at exactly that address.

### The sign-in request expired

Try again. Approve the request in your signer promptly.

### Your role no longer allows this page

Bancwr checks your role on every page. If an administrator changes your access while you have a page open, you see a notice with a link back to the dashboard.
